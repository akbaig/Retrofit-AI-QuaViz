Public-observation physics feature contract
==========================================

add_physics_features(frame, asset) -> 48-column float32 DataFrame, same index.
extract_snapshot_features(samples, sample_rate_hz, rpm, asset) -> 13 floats.

The implementation has no filesystem reads. It uses public assets.csv ratings,
observed minute aggregates, and contemporaneous or past-asof operating context.
Neither asset identifiers, event identifiers, simulator randomness, latent states,
scenario schedules, nor labels enter feature formulas. Physics equations were
checked against public physics.py / plant.py and TECHNICAL_MODEL.md.

Causality: minute aggregate availability is minute completion, not minute start.
The caller must supply one asset's chronological complete minute grid. Sparse
observations can be held only backwards with bounded age, retaining age/missing
features. Thermal expected values have past-only first-order lags (1500/2400 s).
Waveform availability is timestamp + n_samples/sample_rate_hz; join only a fully
captured past waveform, retain its age, and reset/expire around stops if desired.

Missing signals remain NaN. The one physical substitution is fixed-speed motor
speed inferred from observed running state. Healthy-curve-inferred flow has its
own feature name; it is never used to compute the observed head-curve residual
or measured hydraulic efficiency, because that would enforce fake consistency.
NPSH may use observed flow or explicitly curve-inferred flow, so it is a model
estimate and can be biased by pressure sensor faults or degraded pump curves.

Shared nominal assumptions: temperature-control design water 25 C, setpoint from
public context (36 C fallback), equal-percentage valve rangeability 50 and design
position 0.8 with 25% of system resistance; suction velocity 2.5 m/s at BEP;
bearing rise 28 K, winding rise 75 K; electrical thermal load proxy 0.94 of
observed input power, falling back to rated-normalized current. These are broad
engineering normalizers, not asset-specific healthy-state reconstruction. Their
bias should be handled by the learned healthy regressor and causal residuals.

Waveform features use a Hann spectrum and acceleration-to-velocity integration
around measured 1x, 2x, 3x and vane-pass orders. Envelope evidence uses a Butterworth
bandpass (normally 1800-4000 Hz), Hilbert amplitude, then power in the first three
BPFO/BPFI harmonics with bands covering 1 Hz resolution and small bearing slip.
BPFO prominence compares line-band and neighboring-band mean power. Missing or
invalid snapshots yield NaN for all 13 columns.

Snapshots are useful for separating 1x-dominant imbalance, 2x/3x misalignment,
and BPFO-modulated bearing impacts from broadband hydraulic noise. They occur
only every four hours: do not imply continuous spectral diagnosis. The simulator
synthesizes them directly from physical vibration components, independently of
monitoring-channel sensor corruptions. Report this and ablate waveform features
to show their contribution separately from the minutely telemetry architecture.

Validation performed: tests/test_physics.py has five passing tests covering prefix
causality/no mutation, correct healthy curve identity, missing-evidence semantics,
harmonic velocity recovery, BPFO envelope discrimination, and invalid records.
Smoke tested all seven TRAIN assets only (70,494 original minute rows) and one
real public TRAIN snapshot. No validation/test labels or predictions were read.
