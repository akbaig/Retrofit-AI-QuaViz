WHAT WAS BUILT

A supervised boosted-tree ensemble with physical signal processing and a
shared sensor-diagnosis branch; no Isolation Forest. Fourteen healthy-response
regressors provide expected values and residuals. Four equally weighted views
average scores on machine faults/gateway loss; four shared classifiers identify sensor
drift, freezing, calibration shifts and noise by inspecting each channel.
The compact artifact contains 40 active binary classifiers and 14 regressors.

Training union: 7 supplied training pumps plus 108 generated training recordings,
396,720 minute slots. The simulator is used to create labeled training examples
with crossed operating/instrumentation conditions. Labels supervise training;
inference does not receive event labels, deadlines, latent states or asset IDs
as predictors. Current/past observations supply every prediction.

The policy needs 15 consecutive minutes of evidence, applies startup/stopped
inhibition, waits four hours below the recovery threshold before rearming, and
allows one 24-hour reminder. It outputs a suspected fault, score and supporting
measurements. Inspection recommendations are a static text lookup; fault
identification is learned. Scores are not calibrated real-world probabilities.

A small neural alternative was actually trained and tested (79.7799 validation,
66.0545 synthetic development under its bounded policy search), then rejected.
A variational autoencoder is not the selected solution: existing fault labels
allow direct learning of which fault is present.

EARLY WARNING

This detects a developing condition from current evidence. It does not forecast
a failure date or detect a condition before it appears in any measurements.
Deadline lead = event action deadline minus first alert time. It does not mean
lead before onset. On original validation only two detected events have such
deadlines: one alert is early, the other about two minutes late. No general
early-warning-duration claim is made from that small denominator.

REPORTING ERROR AND RECOVERY

The first frozen evaluation completed aggregate scoring and rechecked frozen
inputs, then the original scorer raised a TypeError while making a supplemental
per-asset report for a healthy asset with an empty event table. The initial
model predictions and aggregate report were already saved and were preserved.

A separate reporting script reused those exact predictions and recomputed only
the same three predeclared B1 settings. The aggregate report reproduced exactly.

healthy assets. Per-asset official composites for eventless assets are marked
unavailable, with raw alerts and false episodes reported. No model, feature,
policy, seed, training data or scorer changed. The recovery code, original error
record and full freeze verification are retained under provenance/.
